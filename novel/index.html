The Fire of the Gods

Long ago, a warrior's strength wasn't measured by muscle, but by the Astras. These weren't mere weapons; they were the raw essence of the gods. Imagine a nuclear warhead, then imagine something far worse. These celestial arms didn't just break bodies, they shattered souls.

An Astra couldn't be simply picked up. They were locked by sacred frequencies called Mantras, taught only by Sages to those with the discipline to master them. While many commanded the elements, like the fires of the Agneyastra or the tides of the Varunastra, nothing compared to the Mahastras. The weapons of the Trinity, the Brahmastra, Narayanastra, and Pashupatastra, held the power to unmake the universe itself.

The Architects of Chaos

The Rakshasas

They are not mere 'demons' of folklore; they are the ancient anti-existence. The Rakshasas are entities born from the primal darkness, fueled by a hunger that no flesh can sate. Their lineage is older than the mountains, they're driven by an eternal hatred for Sriman Narayan and their singular ambition is the ultimate blasphemy, to plunge the three worlds into an eternal, cold void. They do not just kill; they unmake.

The mantras are chanting. The sky is bleeding. The bow is drawn. Then the Kali rises.
