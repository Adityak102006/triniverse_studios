Your information, handled with care

Privacy
notice.

This notice explains what personal information may be collected when you contact Triniverse Studios, why it is used, and how to make a privacy request.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Suryansh Srivastava, operator of Triniverse Studios, based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India, is responsible for deciding why and how personal data submitted through this site is processed. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, this role is generally described as the Data Fiduciary. Contact: theart.illeryuniverse@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, and the message you choose to send.
  • Work enquiries: an optional CV or portfolio URL and details you include about your experience or availability.
  • Project enquiries: project briefs, business contact details, and information you choose to include in the message.
  • Novel updates: email address and the fact that you opted in to receive occasional updates.
  • Service/security data: technical details such as delivery time, IP address, or anti-spam information may be processed by our website host or form provider under their service policies.

The contact form accepts text and URLs, not file attachments. Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, passwords, health information, or other sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it through a secure and appropriate process.

3. Why and on what basis we use it

We use enquiry information to reply, discuss or assess a proposed project, consider a specific job or freelance enquiry, protect the forms from abuse, and keep records needed to manage or resolve the enquiry. We use a novel-update email only to send the updates requested through that separate sign-up; it is not consent to unrelated marketing. We do not sell personal data.

Where consent is required, the form will ask for a clear, specific choice before submission. You may withdraw consent by contacting us at theart.illeryuniverse@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully or processing required by law. We will not use a job enquiry as permission to send unrelated promotional messages.

4. Form provider and other recipients

The forms are currently a static integration template. They will not send submissions to Web3Forms until the studio configures its access key. When enabled, the submitted fields are sent over HTTPS to Web3Forms, whose service operator says it processes end-user submissions on the site's operator's behalf. Web3Forms identifies infrastructure providers including Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Hetzner, and Amazon SES; its anti-spam or optional integrations may involve additional providers. We will not enable optional integrations without reviewing their data flows.

Web3Forms' current privacy notice says form submissions may be retained for up to three years by default, with shorter retention settings available on some plans or deletion earlier. Before activation, the studio should choose the shortest suitable retention in its account and delete copies from the provider dashboard and its own inbox when no longer needed. The provider says its infrastructure may process data in multiple regions. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement for current details.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect people or the studio from fraud or security threats, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. We do not authorise providers to use enquiry content for their own advertising.

5. Retention

We keep each enquiry only as long as reasonably needed to reply, evaluate the specific opportunity, maintain necessary business records, and handle legal claims or obligations. Our intended practice is to review and delete inactive enquiry and recruitment material within 12 months after the matter closes, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to resolve a dispute. If you wish us to consider a CV for a later role, we will ask you separately rather than assume permission. Provider-held copies and security logs may follow the provider's published retention periods; we will configure a shorter form-retention period where available and delete earlier when practical.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can ask us to confirm whether we hold your information, provide a summary, correct or update inaccurate details, erase information that is no longer needed, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. You may also raise a grievance about our handling of your information. Under the applicable DPDP provisions, individuals also have a right to nominate another person to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity. Requests can be sent to theart.illeryuniverse@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request and protect your data. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

If a grievance is not resolved, you may use the external escalation route available under applicable law, including the Data Protection Board of India when the relevant statutory provisions are in force and its process is available.

7. Security, children, and external sites

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and the services involved, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your information has been exposed, contact us promptly at the address above. This site and its work-enquiry forms are intended for adults. If you are under 18, do not send a CV or personal details yourself; ask a parent or legal guardian to contact us.

Our site may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. This website currently contains no advertising or analytics scripts; our hosting and configured form/security services may still create operational logs.

8. Updates and legal framework

We may update this notice when our forms, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the current version here and update the date above. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 have staged commencement dates; this notice is intended to support the applicable Indian privacy framework and will be checked against the provisions in force when the forms are activated. Nothing in this notice removes a right provided by applicable Indian law.
